Drop your real photography here using these filenames (see src/data/images.js
to rename or add more):

  hero.jpg              - hero section background/panel
  about.jpg             - about section panel
  coverage.jpg          - football coverage section background
  partnerships.jpg      - partnerships section banner
  featured-1.jpg        - featured content card 1 ("The next generation of Barça")
  featured-2.jpg        - featured content card 2 ("What makes this midfield different?")
  featured-3.jpg        - featured content card 3 ("90 minutes. One story.")
  featured-4.jpg        - featured content card 4 ("The numbers behind the performance")
  featured-5.jpg        - featured content card 5 ("Barça's new era")
  og-cover.jpg          - social share preview image (referenced in index.html)

If a file is missing, the site automatically falls back to a generated
dark gradient panel, so nothing breaks — it just looks best once real
images are in place.

Recommended size: at least 1600px on the long edge, landscape or
portrait depending on the slot, compressed to keep page weight low.
